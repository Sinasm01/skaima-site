Skaima — VIP Purchase and License Terms

Proposed effective date: November 2, 2026
Publisher: Egaurd · sinasalehimilani2016@gmail.com · Telegram: @Elromax

1. Free use and VIP access

Skaima may be installed for free as a classic Figma plugin. As of v3.7.0, Color Studio has a five-day local trial, Typography has a three-day local trial, and Design System Docs is free. Radius & Spacing requires VIP. Layout Studio is marked Coming Soon and is not included in VIP.

2. Price and payment

The advertised VIP price is USD $0.50 for a single 30-day access period. VIP is a one-time purchase and does not renew automatically. Contact Egaurd by email or Telegram before sending money to confirm accepted payment methods, payment instructions, any applicable fees/taxes, and the final amount. Payments are arranged outside Figma and are not processed by Figma or the plugin. Never send full card details or account passwords in messages.

3. Delivery and start of term

After Egaurd manually confirms payment, the purchaser provides the random Installation ID shown in Skaima → VIP Token. Egaurd issues an installation-bound, digitally signed VIP token and sends it through the agreed contact channel. Manual handling is not instantaneous; contact Egaurd if delivery is delayed. The 30-day term begins when the token is issued, not when the purchaser first enters it. The issue and expiry timestamps are contained in the token.

4. Activation and limitations

Paste the token into VIP Token → Verify & activate. A token works only with its designated Installation ID and can be invalid or rejected if expired, mistyped, used on another installation, or affected by local-clock/storage problems. Please contact Egaurd with the original purchase reference if reissue is necessary. The current system uses offline verification and does not support reliable global revocation. The local Revoke Token action only deactivates the token in that installation; it does not automatically cancel a paid term.

5. Renewals and supported features

There is no recurring charge or automatic extension. To renew, contact Egaurd and obtain a newly issued 30-day token after a new payment. Issuing a new token does not automatically cancel the old token or extend its existing expiry date. VIP covers Color Studio, Typography, and Radius & Spacing during the active period; it does not unlock announced but unavailable features.

6. Refunds and consumer rights

Before payment, ask Egaurd about the refund and cancellation process for your location and payment method. To request a refund, report a faulty token or raise a dispute, contact sinasalehimilani2016@gmail.com and include your purchase reference. Requests are handled in accordance with any mandatory consumer protection, digital-content, chargeback, withdrawal, or refund rights that apply. Nothing in these terms removes a mandatory statutory right. A customer should not be required to waive legal rights merely by submitting an Installation ID. Egaurd must confirm any additional withdrawal disclosures or consents required by its applicable jurisdiction before accepting payment.

7. Availability and support

Skaima operates in Figma Desktop and depends on Figma's platform and plugin APIs. Create a backup/duplicate before applying Typography to a full file. Egaurd can offer support through the stated contact channels, but cannot guarantee uninterrupted availability of Figma itself. If the tool fails materially, send a clear reproducible description to support.
