Skaima — Privacy Policy

Proposed effective date: November 2, 2026
Product: Skaima · Design System Studio (Figma plugin)
Publisher/contact: Egaurd · sinasalehimilani2016@gmail.com · Telegram: @Elromax

1. Scope and overview

This policy describes the current Skaima v3.7.0 plugin and manual VIP support process. Skaima is a Figma plugin that creates and manages design-system foundations and documentation. The plugin's published manifest disallows network access. Its normal plugin functions operate inside Figma, and the current release does not automatically transmit your Figma document or design-system variables to an Egaurd server. You can still voluntarily contact Egaurd outside Figma to request VIP access or support.

2. Information stored and processed in Figma

  • Design information: When you run a feature, Skaima reads the relevant local Figma variables, collections, modes, styles, or text layers as required for preview and confirmed operations. Design System Docs creates an editable snapshot Frame inside your Figma document; anyone with access to that file may be able to view its contents.
  • Local plugin preferences and history: Appearance settings, some palette presets, restoration/history data and operation metadata are stored in Figma plugin storage or in the current Figma file, depending on the feature.
  • Installation and licensing: Skaima stores a randomly generated Installation ID, trial dates, local time checks, and any activated VIP/Admin token in Figma clientStorage. It does not request hardware serial numbers, MAC addresses, or hardware fingerprints.
  • Manual VIP: Signed VIP tokens are checked offline within the plugin. The current plugin does not automatically send Installation IDs or tokens to a licensing server.

Figma operates the host platform and may handle information under its own policies. Shared presets and generated documentation in a Figma file are accessible according to that file's sharing permissions.

3. Information you may send to Egaurd

If you request a paid VIP token, you may choose to share your Installation ID, email address or Telegram username, payment confirmation/reference, and related messages with Egaurd. If you request support, you may provide diagnostics, screenshots, or sample files voluntarily. Do not send passwords, card details, private keys, or confidential design files in support messages. Manual payments are arranged outside the plugin; Skaima does not collect card information in its interface.

Email, Telegram, financial institutions, and any payment service you choose are separate service providers with their own privacy practices. Do not assume messages to those providers are stored solely inside Skaima.

4. Uses and legal basis

We use information received voluntarily to respond to inquiries, confirm manually arranged payments, issue or reissue installation-bound VIP tokens, provide support, handle disputes, and meet applicable legal/accounting obligations. Where applicable, processing may be based on fulfilling a customer request or contract, legitimate support/security interests, consent for optional information, or legal obligations. The applicable legal basis depends on your location and the transaction.

5. Sharing and disclosures

We do not sell customer information. Manual communications and payments may be processed by the email, messaging, banking or payment providers selected by the customer and publisher. Information may be disclosed when required by applicable law or to address fraud, abuse, or disputes. The plugin's current release does not include analytics or automatic telemetry to an Egaurd server.

6. Retention

Local plugin information generally stays in Figma storage until changed, cleared, or removed through applicable product/storage actions; we do not control Figma's retention of file content. We retain manual support, license-issuance and payment records only for as long as reasonably necessary for customer service, license administration, disputes, and applicable accounting or legal requirements. We review deletion requests individually and delete information that we no longer need and are not legally required to retain. Publisher action before publication: establish and actually follow a written retention and deletion procedure for manually received records.

7. Security and limitations

We use signed, installation-bound VIP tokens, but verification in the current build is offline and relies on the local clock and plugin storage. It is not a guarantee against tampering or data loss. We aim to limit collection to what is needed to deliver requested services. You are responsible for controlling access to your shared Figma files and for avoiding sending sensitive information through support channels.

8. Your choices and requests

You can use the free features without buying VIP. You can choose not to contact Egaurd or submit payment information; paid activation then will not be available. For questions, access, correction, deletion or other privacy requests, email sinasalehimilani2016@gmail.com. Applicable privacy laws may grant additional rights, including the right to object, restrict certain processing, or lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority.

9. Children, changes and contact

Skaima is intended for users permitted to use Figma and is not designed specifically for children. We may update this policy when the product or manual payment workflow changes; the published page should show the latest effective date. For privacy questions: sinasalehimilani2016@gmail.com or Telegram @Elromax.
